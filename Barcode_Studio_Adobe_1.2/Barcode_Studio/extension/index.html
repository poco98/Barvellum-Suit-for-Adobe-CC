Barcode workflow
  1. Import Reference and Barcode columns in Database.
  2. Find a product reference in Barcode.
  3. Generate, verify and insert into your document.
Use Code 128 for ordinary text or references. EAN/UPC require digits and a valid check digit. The QR link tab is independent of the product table.


Your base measurements
X dimension: 0.40 mm. Bar height: 10 mm. Text: Amazon Ember Regular, 14 px (10.5 pt). Dimensions remain in place when changing products. Local settings are remembered.


Illustrator insertion
The SVG is opened as a temporary document. Its native vectors are duplicated into your active document, grouped and centred on the active artboard. The temporary document is then closed. No raster conversion or clipboard copy is used. Select an unlocked, visible layer.


Print checks
All bars are expanded filled paths. Text remains editable in Illustrator. RGB black is used; check 100% K conversion for CMYK work. The 10 mm base height is your layout setting and is not a certification of GS1 minimum dimensions. Verify the final print separately.


QR and link checks
QR reading confirms that the image decodes to your link. Link availability checks the server response where possible. A login page, blocked request, timeout or CORS restriction is reported separately. A successful response does not certify the page content or future availability.


Data and compatibility
UTF-8 CSV/TSV, XLSX and JSON are supported. Store codes as text to retain zeros. Existing local product data is retained on update. The panel is configured for Windows Illustrator 29–31, Photoshop 26–28 and InDesign 20–22. Run Install.cmd with all three apps closed.
