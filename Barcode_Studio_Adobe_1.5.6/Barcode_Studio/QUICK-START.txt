BARCODE STUDIO

1. Extract the entire ZIP file to a folder.
2. Close Illustrator, Photoshop and InDesign.
3. Run Install.cmd.
4. Reopen the Adobe app and choose Window > Extensions > Barcode Studio.
   In Photoshop, choose Window > Extensions (Legacy) > Barcode Studio.

To remove the panel, close the Adobe apps and run Uninstall.cmd.

The Resources folder includes the panel, documentation and a sample CSV file.
